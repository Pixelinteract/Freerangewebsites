3. The Build Process

3.1 Intake Form

The build begins when you complete our intake form in full. This form collects your business name, services, primary suburb and service areas, contact details, logo, and any photos you wish to use. The accuracy and completeness of this information is your responsibility. Delays in completing the intake form delay the build — our quoted turnaround times begin from the date we receive a complete submission.

3.2 Turnaround Time

Most websites are live within 7–21 business days of receiving a complete intake form. This is a guideline, not a guarantee. Turnaround depends on prompt client communication and timely feedback during the revision process.

3.3 Page Scope

Each plan includes a fixed number of pages as specified in your plan details (Starter: 3 pages, Standard: 5 pages). A page is defined as a distinct URL with its own content.

Each page is scoped to a maximum of 6 content sections. A content section is a distinct visual block — for example: a hero banner, a services list, a testimonials block, or a call-to-action. Content that exceeds 6 sections per page is outside the build scope and will be treated as a content update request post-launch, or quoted separately.

Additional pages beyond your plan's page allowance are not included and must be quoted separately.

3.4 Build Revision Rounds

Two (2) revision rounds are included during the build phase, prior to sign-off.

A revision round is defined as:

  • One consolidated list of change requests submitted in a single email or message
  • All requested changes must be submitted together — staggered feedback sent across multiple days does not constitute a single round and will be held until collated and resubmitted as one

Round 1: We send you a draft. You review it and send back all your feedback in one message.

Round 2: We apply your feedback. You review again and either sign off or submit a final consolidated list of remaining changes.

If further changes are requested after two revision rounds and prior to sign-off, each additional round is charged at $75 per round, invoiced separately before work commences.

Once you provide sign-off via clear written confirmation, the build phase closes. Any further changes after sign-off are treated as content update requests under your subscription.

3.5 Creative Direction and Design

Free Range Websites retains creative discretion over layout, typography, colour application, and visual design decisions. We will work with your brand colours and assets, but we do not offer open-ended design customisation or redesigns based on subjective preference after the revision rounds are exhausted.

Requests that amount to redesigning the site from scratch — rather than refining the existing design — are outside scope and will be declined or quoted separately.

3.6 Abandoned Builds

If we are unable to progress the build due to non-response from you, the following applies:

  • After 21 days without a response to our communications, the build is placed on hold
  • After 60 days without a response, the job is closed and the setup fee is retained by Free Range Websites to cover work completed
  • If you wish to recommence after a job has been closed, a new setup fee applies

5.1 What a Content Update Is

A content update is a change to existing content on your live website. Examples include: updating your phone number, changing service descriptions, swapping a photo, adding a testimonial, or amending your service area list.

5.2 What Is Not a Content Update

The following are not content updates and are not included in any subscription plan:

  • Adding new pages to the site
  • Redesigning the layout or structure of existing pages
  • Adding new sections beyond the original scoped structure
  • Changing the overall visual design, colour scheme, or typography
  • Logo design, graphic design, or any design work
  • Building new functionality (booking systems, e-commerce, custom forms)

5.3 What Constitutes a Round

A content update round is one batch of change requests submitted in a single, consolidated email or message. Requests submitted across multiple messages over several days do not constitute a single round — they will be held and counted as a new round when resubmitted together.

We will not begin work on a partial or staggered list.

Starter plan subscribers receive 2 content update rounds per month. Rounds reset at the start of each billing cycle — unused rounds do not carry over. If both rounds are used before the billing cycle ends, further requests will be held until the next cycle.

5.5 Standard Plan — Unlimited Updates (Fair Use)

Standard plan subscribers receive unlimited content update rounds, subject to fair use. Fair use means updates are for maintaining and improving existing content — not for continuous redesign or scope expansion. Requests should be batched and consolidated before submission.

5.6 Turnaround on Content Updates

We aim to complete standard content update requests within 3–5 business days of receiving a complete, consolidated request. Turnaround is not guaranteed and may vary during peak periods.
