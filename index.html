Questions answered

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I own my website?+
The website code remains ours while you're on a subscription — that's how we keep the monthly cost low. Think of it like leasing a ute instead of buying one outright. You get full use of it, we handle the maintenance. After 12 months on a plan you can purchase the code outright if you want to self-host.
What does "unlimited content updates" actually mean?+
Text changes, photo swaps, new services, updated pricing, changed phone number — as many times as you like. What it doesn't include is building completely new pages, redesigning the site, or logo work. We'll always be clear about what's in scope.
Is my domain included?+
Domain pointing is included — if you already own a domain, we'll connect it to your new site for free. If you don't have a domain yet, we can register one for you for $35/yr. You always stay in control of your domain name.
Can I cancel? What happens to my site?+
After your initial 6-month period, you can cancel with 30 days notice — no hard feelings, no interrogation. When you cancel, we give you a copy of all the files so nothing is ever lost.
How long does setup actually take?+
From the moment you fill in our intake form, your site is live within 3 business days. The form takes about 5 minutes — we ask for your logo, services, suburb, a few photos, and your contact details. We'll send you a link to review before it goes live.
Why not just use Wix or build it myself?+
You can — plenty of tradies do. But building something that looks decent takes 15–20 hours of your time. At your hourly rate on the tools, that's a lot of money. DIY templates also aren't built for local SEO. And who's updating it when you're on the job? With FreeRange you send a message and it's sorted.
Will my site actually show up on Google?+
We build every site with local SEO baked in — suburb-targeted copy, structured data markup, fast load times, and Google Business Profile setup included. Most clients see ranking improvements within 6–12 weeks. We'll also set up Google Analytics so you can see exactly where your traffic comes from.
