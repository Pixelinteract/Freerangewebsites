The stuff you're probably wondering

Do you only work with businesses in certain Melbourne suburbs?+
No — we build for businesses right across Melbourne, and Australia-wide. We're featuring certain suburbs first, but where you are doesn't limit whether we can build for you.
Do I need to meet you in person?+
No. Everything happens by phone, email and a short online form, so it's just as easy whether you're in Glen Waverley or on the other side of the city.
Will this help local customers find me?+
We build the foundation — a fast site, structured properly, with your Google Business Profile set up. We can't promise page one, and nobody can promise that, but without that foundation nothing else has a chance. A lot of those local searches end in “near me”, and it’s the suburb pages and the Business Profile that decide whether you come up in them.

Most were built once, years ago, by someone who's now unreachable — and your business has moved on since. If adding a page means chasing a developer and paying by the hour, it never gets done. Yours would be built to change.
What's the catch?+
There isn't one, but here's our business model in plain English: the build is free, and we make our money from the Care Plan once you're live. We only get paid if you love the site — which is exactly the incentive you want us to have.
Is it really free?+
The build is genuinely free — no setup fee, no deposit, no card details. The Care Plan (hosting, updates, upkeep) is $199/month, and it only starts when your site goes live.
I've been searching for cheap website design in Melbourne — is this it?+
If cheap means low cost, then yes. The build is free and the monthly fee is $199 — no lock-in, no setup cost. If cheap means low quality, then no — every site is hand-coded, mobile-first, and built to be found on Google.
What if I don't like the site?+
You walk away. No payment, no invoice, no hard feelings. And before that, you get a full 7-day review window with unlimited changes — batch your feedback into a list and we'll work through the lot.

Ready to review within 5 business days of your completed form — or your first month is free. Once you approve and pay, it's live within 24 hours.
Am I locked into a contract?+
No. Month to month from day one, cancel anytime with 30 days notice. Watch out for "free website" offers that need a 12-month contract — that's not free, that's a lease with a smile.
