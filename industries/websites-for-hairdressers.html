Let's talk about what's going wrong

Why nobody's finding your website

You're good at what you do. That's not the problem. The problem is what happens before anyone books.

Your site takes too long to load, so people give up and book somewhere else.

More than half of people looking on their phone will leave if a page takes longer than three seconds to come up. Someone looking for a cut before the weekend isn't waiting — they close the tab and try the next salon. Usual cause is a page full of big photos nobody bothered to shrink down.

We hand-code every site and keep the images sharp but light, so yours loads fast and still looks good.

People can't book without ringing, and a lot of them won't ring.

Plenty of your customers would rather book at 10pm on their phone than call during the day. If the only way to get in is a phone number and opening hours, you're quietly losing every one of them.

We put online booking front and centre on every page, so people can book the moment they decide. Being set up as a proper registered business helps you look the part, and business.gov.au covers the basics.

Google doesn't know what you actually specialise in.

If your site just says "hair salon," Google has nothing to go on. It can't tell whether you do colour, extensions, men's cuts, or bridal. So when someone nearby searches for one of those, Google shows them somebody else — because somebody else spelled it out.

We give each service its own page, so Google knows exactly what to show you for.

Your work is on Instagram and nowhere else.

Your best colour work lives on a feed that disappears down the timeline in a week, and Google can't see any of it. Meanwhile someone searching for a colourist in your suburb lands on a website with a stock photo and no proof you're any good.

We bring your best work onto your own site where it stays put and can actually be found.

You're paying booking platforms a cut of customers who should be yours.

Every booking that comes through a platform costs you a slice, and the customer belongs to them. They'll show your competitors alongside you and take your regulars if you ever leave. You're renting customers who ought to be yours already.

Your own site, your own bookings, one flat monthly. Nobody takes a cut.

We won't promise you page one of Google. Nobody can. What we build is the foundation — fast, structured properly, a page for everything you offer. Without that, nothing else has a chance of working.

Something worth thinking about

Your best work is on Instagram, and Google can't see a single bit of it.

Almost every salon has the same setup. The Instagram is full of great colour work, before-and-afters, happy clients. The website, if there is one, has a stock photo of a woman in a towel and a phone number from three years ago. Everything good is on a platform that buries it within a week.

The problem is that Instagram and Google do completely different jobs. Instagram shows your work to people who already follow you. Google shows you to someone who's just moved into the suburb, typed "balayage near me," and has never heard of you. That second person is new business, and right now they're landing on a site that gives them no reason to choose you.

You don't have to stop posting. But your best twenty photos belong somewhere permanent, on your own site, where Google can find them and where a new client can see exactly what you do before they book.

That's the gap we close. Send us your best work, it goes on your site, and it stays there working for you instead of disappearing down a feed.

That's why updates are unlimited and included. Ring us, tell us what you're doing now, and it's on your site.
