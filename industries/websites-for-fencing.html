Let's talk about what's going wrong

Why nobody's finding your website

You're a good fencer. That's not the problem. The problem is what happens before anyone picks up the phone.

Your site takes too long to load, so people give up and ring someone else.

More than half of people looking on their phone will leave if a page takes longer than three seconds to come up. Someone standing in their backyard looking at a fence that's fallen over is not waiting — they hit back and call the next fencer on the list. Usual cause is a giant photo on the front page that nobody bothered to shrink down.

We hand-code every site and keep the files small, so yours comes up fast.

People can't tell what fencing materials you actually do.

Colorbond, timber, glass pool fencing, aluminium, retaining — these are completely different jobs and people search for them by name. If your site just says "fencing," a customer who wants a glass pool fence can't tell if you're the right call, so they ring someone who said it plainly.

We lay out the fencing types you install clearly, so the right customer knows they've found the right fencer. Looking like a registered, insured contractor helps too, and business.gov.au lays out what that takes.

Google doesn't know what kind of fencing you do.

If your site just says "fencing services," Google has nothing to go on. It can't tell whether you do Colorbond, timber, pool fencing or gates. So when someone nearby searches for one of those, Google shows them somebody else — because somebody else spelled it out.

We give each type of fencing its own page, so Google knows exactly what to show you for.

Pool fencing rules make people nervous, and your site says nothing about it.

Anyone putting in a pool fence knows it has to meet safety requirements, and they're worried about getting it wrong. If your site doesn't reassure them you know the rules and do it properly, they'll ring someone who does.

We make your experience and compliance clear, so nervous customers feel safe calling you.

You're paying for leads you should be getting yourself.

Every lead you buy off a directory is a lead three other fencers bought at the same time. You're bidding against them on price, you're paying for the privilege, and the day you stop paying, the leads stop dead. You're renting customers who ought to be yours already.

Your own site, your own leads, one flat monthly. Nobody takes a cut.

We won't promise you page one of Google. Nobody can. What we build is the foundation — fast, structured properly, a page for every job you do. Without that, nothing else has a chance of working.

Something worth thinking about

Half your customers ring because something broke, and the other half have been thinking about it for months.

Fencing is unusual because you're really serving two completely different customers. One has just had a fence come down in a storm or found the neighbour's tree through their palings — they want it sorted this week and they're ringing whoever picks up. The other has been planning a new Colorbond fence or a pool surround for months, comparing materials and prices, and they'll read everything before they call.

Most fencing websites only serve one of those. They're either a bare phone number with no detail, which loses the planner, or a wall of text with the number buried, which loses the emergency. You want both.

A site that works for your trade puts your number one tap away for the person with a broken fence, and gives the planner real detail on materials, styles and finished jobs so they can picture their own place. Same site, two jobs, and most of your competitors are only doing one of them.

That's what we build — fast enough for the urgent call, detailed enough for the slow decision.
