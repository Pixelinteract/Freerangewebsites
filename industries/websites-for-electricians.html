Let's talk about what's going wrong

Why nobody's finding your website

You're a good sparky. That's not the problem. The problem is what happens before anyone picks up the phone.

Your site takes too long to load, so people give up and ring someone else.

More than half of people looking on their phone will leave if a page takes longer than three seconds to come up. They're not sitting there waiting for you — they hit back and call the next sparky on the list. Usual cause is a giant photo on the front page that nobody bothered to shrink down.

We hand-code every site and keep the files small, so yours comes up fast.

Your website makes you look like a handyman, not a licensed electrician.

You've done the training. You've got the licence. But if someone lands on your site and can't see that in a few seconds, you look like every bloke with a ute and a set of tools. People get nervous about electrical work. They want to know you're the real thing before they ring.

We put your licence and your credentials where people actually look.

Google doesn't know what kind of electrical work you do.

If your site just says "electrical services," Google has nothing to go on. It can't tell whether you do switchboard upgrades, or rewires, or solar, or EV chargers. So when someone nearby searches for one of those, Google shows them somebody else — because somebody else spelled it out.

We give each of your services its own page, so Google knows exactly what to show you for.

The jobs go to whoever's easiest to find and quickest to ring.

When someone needs a sparky, they search, they look at the first two or three, and they call. That's it. They're not comparing quotes or reading your About page. If your number isn't right there on the screen, ready to tap, you're not in the running — no matter how good you are.

Your phone number sits at the top of every page, one tap away, on any device.

You're paying hipages a cut of every job you should have had for nothing.

Every lead you buy is a lead three other sparkies bought at the same time. You're bidding against them on price, you're paying for the privilege, and the day you stop paying, the leads stop dead. You're renting customers who ought to be yours already.

Your own site, your own leads, one flat monthly. Nobody takes a cut.

We won't promise you page one of Google. Nobody can. What we build is the foundation — fast, structured properly, a page for every job you do. Without that, nothing else has a chance of working.

The work you do

Every job you do should have its own page

That's how people search, and it's how Google decides who to show them.

Switchboard upgrades

This is the one people search by name, usually the week after a sparky's told them their board is on the way out. A page that explains what an upgrade involves, roughly what it costs, and why an old board is a fire risk does most of the convincing before they've even rung you.

Rewires

Nobody rings about a rewire on a whim. They think about it for weeks and they compare three sparkies properly. That's a good thing — it means there's room to explain what's involved, how long they'll be living around it, and what it does for the house. Most sites give it one line.

Solar and battery

Big jobs, good money, and the questions people have are all the same ones. How many panels, where does the battery go, will it actually save anything. Answer those on a page and you've done the first half of the sales conversation without leaving the ute.

EV charger installation

The fastest-growing job on the books and hardly anyone's website mentions it. People are searching for it right now and ringing whoever comes up. More on this further down.

Safety checks and compliance

Rentals have to be checked on a regular cycle, and the agent looking after them just wants a sparky who'll answer the phone and do it properly. It's steady, repeat work that comes back every year — but only if your site says you do it.

Commercial and shopfits

Bigger jobs, better margins, and a completely different customer. Most sparky sites look purely residential without meaning to, and quietly tell every builder and business owner who lands on them to go elsewhere.

Your free website includes five pages, so we'll build out the jobs that win you the most work — and you can add more later whenever you like.

Something worth thinking about

EV chargers are the fastest-growing job going, and most electricians' websites don't even mention them.

Every second driveway is getting an EV. Every one of those cars needs a charger put in by a licensed electrician. It's clean work, it pays well, and once you've done one on a street you tend to do a few more.

Now go and look at ten electrician websites around your area and count how many of them mention EV chargers. It'll be one, maybe two. The rest are still listing the same six services they listed in 2015. People are searching for it right now, and Google is handing those jobs to whoever bothered to say they do it.

It's not laziness. Most sparkies' sites were built once, years ago, by someone who's since gone quiet. Adding a service means tracking down a developer, waiting a fortnight, and paying by the hour — so it doesn't get done. The trade moves on and the website stays where it was.

Which is really the point. It won't be EV chargers forever. Something else will come along, and the year it does, your website should say so within a week — not whenever you get around to chasing someone about it.

That's why updates are unlimited and included. Ring us, tell us what you're doing now, and it's on your site.
