Google Business Profile vs. a website — what does a tradie actually need?

Why the two do different jobs, and what happens to your leads if you're only running one of them.

Here's the short of it

Why this isn't an either-or question

A Google Business Profile gets you into the local map pack and reviews with almost no setup — but it's not a website, and it caps what you can show visitors. A website gives you room to explain what you do, showcase past work, and rank for specific searches beyond the map pack. Most trade businesses need both: the profile for visibility and trust signals, the website for everything the profile can't hold.

The gist

Key takeaways

✓A Google Business Profile is free and gets you into the map pack; a website is where people go to actually learn about your business.
✓The map pack shows three results — everyone else on a profile alone is invisible regardless of how good their listing looks.
✓A website lets you rank for searches beyond your business name, and hold far more information than a profile card ever could.

What a Google Business Profile actually does

A Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is the free listing that shows up in the "map pack" — the three results with a map, star rating, and phone number, above the regular search results. It's usually the first thing someone sees when they search for a trade nearby, and it's the fastest way to show reviews, opening hours, and a phone number without building anything.

What a website actually does

A website is where someone goes to check you're legitimate, see examples of past work, understand your specific services, and read enough to decide you're the one to call — not just one of three logos on a map. It's also the only one of the two that can rank for searches beyond your exact business name, since a profile has a fixed format Google controls, not one you can shape yourself.

Why you need both, not just one

A profile with no website works until someone wants more than a phone number — they can't see past jobs, read about a specific service, or confirm you cover their suburb before they call. A website with no profile has the opposite problem: it might be a genuinely good site, but it's missing the map pack placement that's often the first thing a local searcher sees, along with the reviews people check before calling a tradie they've never used.

What happens to your leads if you're only running one

Profile only: you're relying entirely on three map pack spots and whatever a searcher can gather from photos and reviews — if a competitor with a website ranks alongside you, they're giving searchers a reason to pick them that you can't match. Website only: you're invisible in the map pack and missing the reviews and instant contact details that convince someone to call before they've even opened a website. Either way, you're handing an advantage to competitors running both.

Setting them up to work together

The two need to point at each other consistently — same business name, phone number, and address on both, and a link from the profile straight to the site. If the services listed on your profile don't have a matching page on your website, that's a mismatch worth fixing too. Questions worth asking

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a website if I already have a Google Business Profile?+
Yes, in most cases. A profile can get you found in the map pack, but it can't hold examples of your work, explain your specific services, or rank for searches beyond your business name the way a website can.
Does having a website help my Google Business Profile perform better?+
A consistent, linked website is one of several signals Google weighs for local results. It won't override everything else, but a mismatched or missing website is one less reason for Google to trust the listing.
Can I just use my Google Business Profile instead of a website?+
You can, and some very small operations do. The trade-off is that you're limited to Google's format, you can't show much beyond photos and reviews, and you're relying entirely on the map pack rather than also ranking in regular search results.
How do I make sure my profile and website work together?+
Keep your business name, phone number, and address identical on both, link your website from the profile, and make sure the services listed on your profile actually have a matching page on the site.
