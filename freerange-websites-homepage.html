Questions answered

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I own my website? +
The website code remains ours while you're on a subscription — that's how we keep the monthly cost low. Think of it like leasing a ute instead of buying one outright. You get full use of it, we handle the maintenance. After 12 months on a plan, you can purchase the code outright if you want to self-host. We'll always be upfront about the buyout price before you sign up.
What does "unlimited content updates" actually mean? +
It means you can send us requests to update text, swap photos, add new services, update your pricing, change your phone number — as many times as you like. What it doesn't include is building completely new pages, redesigning the site, or logo work. Those are separate conversations. We'll always be clear about what's in and what's out.
Is my domain included? +
Domain pointing is included — meaning if you already own a domain, we'll connect it to your new site for free. If you don't have a domain yet, we can register one for you for $35/yr (that includes .com.au). We keep domain registration separate so you're never locked out of your own domain name, no matter what happens.
Can I cancel? What happens to my site? +
After your initial 6-month period, you can cancel with 30 days notice. No hard feelings, no interrogation. When you cancel, your site comes down — but we'll always give you a copy of the files, so nothing is lost. If you later want to reinstate your subscription, we can have you back online quickly.
How long does setup actually take? +
From the moment you fill in our intake form, your site is live within 3 business days. The intake form takes about 5 minutes — we'll ask for your logo, services, suburb, a few photos, and your contact details. That's genuinely all we need. We'll send you a link to review the site before it goes live, so you can give us any final tweaks.
Why not just use Wix or Squarespace? +
You can — plenty of tradies do. But there are three issues: First, building something that looks decent takes 15–20 hours of your time. At your hourly rate on the tools, that's a lot of money. Second, DIY template builders aren't built for local SEO — they're built to look nice, not to rank in Google. Third, who's updating it when you're on the job? With FreeRange, you send a message and it's sorted. That's the difference.
Will my website actually show up on Google? +
We build every site with local SEO baked in — suburb-targeted copy, structured data markup, fast load times, and Google Business Profile setup. Results vary by trade and location, but most of our clients see Google ranking improvements within 6–12 weeks. We'll also set up Google Analytics so you can see exactly where your traffic is coming from.
